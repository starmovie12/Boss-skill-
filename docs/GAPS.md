# Gaps in the sources (PH1). Steers the field study. Ids are ledger unit ids.

## WEB
- No measured verification: no step renders the page at several widths, reads the console, measures overlap, contrast or bundle size; every gate is self-attested (S1-2938, S2-P03-1148, S3-B-0230).
- Responsive method is thumb-first mobile only in S1/S2 (S2-P03-0989, S1-2071); S3 has ten responsive checks but fixed device breakpoints (S3-F:references/layout-platform.md).
- No content, information-architecture, SEO, social-card or structured-data method; landing pages are frozen into section templates (S3-F:references/page-patterns.md, S1-2498).
- One framework locked as law (S1-1170, S2-P06-1910); no stack decision tied to free hosting a phone user can deploy to.
- No demo-data method (plausible, seeded, labeled) and no tool-run stub scan (S1-0696 bans placeholders by assertion only).
## APP
- No method to choose navigation architecture (tabs, stack, drawer, depth) (S3-B-0194); tablet and foldable are one line.
- Permissions appear only as a "denied" test case: no request timing, rationale strings or recovery (S2-P23-4359, S2-P06-1839).
- No store-readiness (icons, splash, privacy policy, data-safety declarations, signing, review rejections) (S1-2769 stops at config).
- Phone-only playbook covers build and ship but not on-device testing or debugging (S2-P06-1949).
- Locked to one stack and one product's chat-shaped native-feel list (S2-P25-5351, S2-P03-1057).
## PROMPT
- S1 and S3 have nothing on writing prompts or work orders beyond templates addressed to themselves (S1-3277, S3-B-0305).
- S2 has prompt order and requirement tests (S2-P05-1731, S2-P08-2441) but no stop rules, scope limits, anti-overbuild rule, adversarial self-review, budget-vs-work check or target-platform size limits.
## AUDIT
- Shapes exist only for security reports and spec audits (S1-2603, S2-P09-2627, S2-P18-3462); no single schema across security, accessibility, performance, SEO, correctness, privacy, UX.
- No verifiable citation (quoted snippet checked at file:line), no confidence label, no "not checked" section, no coverage matrix, no fix re-verification (all reviews).
- Lenses are tuned to social, economy and realtime products; nothing for plain correctness or measured performance (S2-P04-1239 to S2-P04-1371).
## Design variety
- S1 mandates one house look and one navigation skin (S1-2071, S1-2410); S1/S2 offer a fixed menu of named directions (S1-2901, S2-P05-1698); S3 picks palettes, fonts and styles from tables by product type (see s3b), so equal briefs give equal looks.
- No domain study of real products, no randomized divergence, no cross-project distinctiveness check; the anti-generic audit judges one design against templates, never against earlier projects (S3-B-0148).
## Verification
- Every gate is silent or self-graded, scorecards are graded by the maker, "passed" is claimed from mental simulation (S1-2340, S2-P04-1510, S3-F:references/scorecard-and-evidence.md).
- No geometry checker for placement (overlap, off-screen, covered primary action, tap size); S3 ships contrast and glyph scripts only.
## Placement
- No single occlusion rule ("nothing covers the primary action or a required control"); coverage rules are scattered over keyboard, safe areas, sticky insets and focus (S3 review).
- No conventional-position rule for close, search, back and account controls outside one product's canon (S2-P03-0989 has back top-left; S1-2410 is product-specific).
## Token cost and process
- Always-loaded prose: S1 about 3,770 lines, S2 about 5,960 lines (about 340 lines of wrappers), S3 Brain 74 KB; laws restated three to five times (all reviews).
- S1/S2 have no task-size routing (S3 does), no clarifying-question budget, and halt-and-wait protocols that stall an unattended run (S2-P00-0194, S2-P10-2688, S3-B-0055).
- No brownfield sampling rule for a repo too large to read whole (S2-P01-0504 says read everything).
